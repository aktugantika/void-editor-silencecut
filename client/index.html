How to use
1. Place a linked video+audio clip on V1/A1 or choose a diffirent cut mode.
2. Adjust settings or pick a preset.
3. Click Generate Preview to visualize cuts.
4. Click Run SilenceCut to apply.
🖱 Scroll on waveform to zoom.
🖱 Drag waveform to pan.
🖱 Double-click to reset zoom.
